Admissions open for Term 1, 2027

Announcements 2 November 2026

Troas Primary School is now receiving applications for Term 1, 2027, from Baby Class to Primary Seven, for both day and boarding learners.

Parents and guardians can complete the online application form on our Admissions page. Our office will contact you to arrange a visit and the next steps. Please check the Fees page for the current fee structure and payment guide.
