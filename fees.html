Uniform and personal items

Parents should obtain the current approved uniform list and supplier details from the school office before purchasing. Typical items include:

  • School uniform and house/sports wear
  • Black school shoes and appropriate socks
  • School bag, exercise books and stationery
  • Personal hygiene items for boarding learners

Uniform prices and item requirements may change. Confirm the latest list with the school office.
