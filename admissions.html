Entry requirements

  • Completed online application or school application form.
  • Copy of the learner’s birth certificate or other age-verification document.
  • Previous school report, where applicable.
  • Transfer documents for learners joining from another school.
  • Parent or guardian contact details and a visit/interview when requested.

The school office will confirm the current age/class placement and any additional documents before admission.

Before you apply

  • Choose the class your child is joining.
  • Have a phone number the office can reach you on.
  • Review the fee structure.
  • We will invite you to visit the school.
