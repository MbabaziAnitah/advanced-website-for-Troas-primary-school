Our story

Our history

Troas Primary School was established in 2002 with a vision to provide quality education that is accessible to all. Starting with just 50 learners and 5 teachers, we have grown into a vibrant community of over 800 learners and 60 staff members.

Over the years we have expanded our facilities to include a computer lab, a library and extensive sports grounds, while keeping our commitment to the growth of every individual child.

Our mission

To provide a nurturing and inclusive learning environment that empowers learners to achieve academic excellence, develop strong character and become responsible citizens.

Our vision

To be a leading educational institution recognised for innovation, excellence and the holistic development of every child, preparing them for the challenges of the future.
